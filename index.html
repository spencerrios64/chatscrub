chatscrub

Remove API keys, emails, phone numbers and other private details from AI chats before you share or archive them. Everything happens in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

What to remove

Scrub export files

Pick AI export files (.zip or .json) or plain text files. The scrubbed exports keep their format: unzip the result and its .json files still load in chatzip and the other tools.

Or scrub pasted text

What it can and can't catch

  • It works by pattern. It catches things with a recognisable shape: keys from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, GitHub, Groq, Slack, AWS and Hugging Face, private key blocks, login tokens, emails, card numbers and phone numbers.
  • It cannot know that a word is a name, an address or a secret password. Add those to the list above.
  • Check the result yourself before you publish anything. A key that was ever pasted into a chat should be replaced with a new one anyway.

Part of the same family as chatvault, chatzip and chatrelay.
